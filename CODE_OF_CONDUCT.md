# Code of Conduct

This is a small hobby project and everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not programmers, and English or Italian may not be their first language.
- Stay on topic: issues and pull requests are for the app, its bugs and its features.
- Give feedback on the idea or the code, not on the person.
- Accept that the maintainer may decline a request, and that others may disagree with you.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising or anything unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Kingly26). Reports are kept confidential.

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
